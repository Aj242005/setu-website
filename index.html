Before the ride

A map that stays with you.

Delhi & NCR maps are stored on your phone. Plan a route, choose Walking or Two-wheeler, and get a good GPS fix before recording.

Along the way

One trip. Multiple signals.

Record GPS, accelerometer and gyroscope measurements together. A fixed phone position and a clear account of the ride make the data more useful.

After you arrive

Keep the evidence.

Save the trip, review it and explicitly export a training bundle. GPS-withheld replay compares estimates with recorded fixes without feeding those fixes to the estimator.

Evidence before promises

The gaps matter.
We count them.

Our target is at least 90% of GPS-free position updates within 10 metres. Missing estimates count as failures. That target is not yet met.

Install and make your first recording
  1. Download the APK. Only allow installation from a source you trust.
  2. Open SETU and allow location and sensor-related permissions when requested.
  3. Choose the correct activity. Keep Location on, fix the phone securely and record your trip.
  4. Stop safely, then choose Stop & save. Review the trip before exporting.
